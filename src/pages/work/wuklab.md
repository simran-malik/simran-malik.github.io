## What I worked on

* Evaluated web sources cited by 12 AI systems on 8 quality metrics across 100 diverse questions.
* Proposed the research direction and the human-labeled ground truth, and built the evaluation pipeline behind the main results.
* Led the Google vs. AI-search study: top AI systems cite sources largely absent from Google's top results (16% overlap for GPT-5 vs. 55% for Tavily).
* Caught a prompt-bias bug that would have skewed every system's results.
